Frequently Asked Questions

What types of machinery do you service?

We specialize in compact spinning systems, modern textile machinery, and industrial automation equipment from leading manufacturers including Rieter, Trützschler, Lakshmi, Savio, and others.

What's your typical turnaround time?

Most repairs are completed within 24-48 hours. For complex jobs, we provide daily updates and work to minimize your downtime wherever possible.

Do you offer preventive maintenance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive preventive maintenance plans customized to your machinery and production schedule to minimize unexpected breakdowns.

Are spare parts always in stock?

We maintain inventory of commonly needed parts and can source specialized components quickly through our supplier network across India.

Do you provide warranty on repairs?

All repairs come with a 6-month warranty on parts and labor. Extended warranties and maintenance contracts are also available.
